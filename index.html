About

Researcher in Secure AI, Cybersecurity, and Health Informatics

Md. Wahidur Rahman is a Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science at Texas A&M University - Kingsville with a research focus on cybersecurity in health informatics. His work connects artificial intelligence, deep learning, computer vision, Internet of Things, and privacy-preserving machine learning.

His research background includes medical image analysis, intelligent IoT systems, agricultural AI, data privacy, federated learning, intrusion detection, and AI-based decision support. He has published journal articles, conference papers, and book chapters in areas related to computer vision, healthcare AI, IoT, and security-focused intelligent systems.
